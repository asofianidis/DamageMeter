# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SpiritVale players who want an at-a-glance account of their own and their party's combat performance without leaving the game or managing configuration files.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable, client-side damage meter that appears only during active play, attributes damage accurately, and exposes its player-facing settings through SpiritVale's centralized Mod Configuration Menu.

## Brand Personality

Native, restrained, dependable. The meter should feel like a quiet SpiritVale utility that supports combat awareness without competing with the game.

## Anti-references

Avoid detached debug windows, raw configuration editors, oversized raid overlays, persistent UI on login screens, decorative animation, noisy diagnostics in normal play, and controls that conflict with SpiritVale's settings patterns.

## Design Principles

1. Keep combat information readable at a glance.
2. Stay out of the way when the player is navigating menus or not in the world.
3. Reuse SpiritVale's visual language and centralized settings flow.
4. Persist player choices immediately and preserve existing configuration values.
5. Fail locally so display or attribution problems never destabilize game startup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep labels and values legible at common resolutions, retain usable scaling limits, avoid relying on color alone, expose clear hotkey descriptions, allow capture cancellation with Escape, and keep all controls usable without editing files.
